# South Jersey Soccer League Official Email Account Policy

## Purpose

To protect South Jersey Soccer League (SJSL) communications, records, and organizational data, all elected and appointed league officials shall be issued an official SJSL email account for conducting league business.

## Policy

Effective July 1, 2026, all individuals serving in elected or appointed positions within SJSL must use their assigned @sjsl.org email account for official league communications.

This requirement applies to, but is not limited to:

- Executive Board members
- Commissioners
- Committee Chairs
- Committee Members conducting official league business
- Appointed league officials
- Any individual authorized to communicate on behalf of SJSL

## Personal and Club Email Accounts

SJSL will no longer forward email to personal, employer, or club-managed email accounts.

Forwarding of official SJSL communications to external accounts creates risks including:

- Unauthorized access to league information
- Loss of administrative control over league records
- Exposure of confidential communications during leadership transitions
- Accidental retention of league information after a volunteer's term ends

For example, if a club-owned email account changes ownership following a club election, SJSL communications previously delivered to that account may become accessible to individuals who are not authorized to receive league information.

## Google Workspace Services

Each official account will be provided through Google Workspace and may include access to:

- Gmail
- Google Drive
- Google Docs
- Google Sheets
- Google Calendar
- Other league-approved collaboration tools

These services are provided for official SJSL business.

## Account Ownership

All @sjsl.org accounts, email messages, files, and associated data remain the property of South Jersey Soccer League.

Users should have no expectation of personal ownership of league accounts or league-owned data.

## Account Transitions

Upon resignation, removal, replacement, or completion of a term of service:

- Access to the account may be suspended or removed.
- League records and documents will remain under SJSL control.
- Successors may be granted access to appropriate records as determined by the Executive Board.

## Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must be approved by the SJSL Executive Board.

## Effective Date

This policy becomes effective July 1, 2026.
